Research Domain

Problem, Gap & Solution

Agriculture faces growing challenges from climate change, pest infestations, and declining soil quality. This section explores the research background, identified gaps, and the solution our team proposes.

🔍 Research Problem ▼

Agriculture faces growing challenges due to climate change, pest infestations, and declining soil quality, which directly affect crop productivity and farmers' livelihoods.

Traditional farming methods often rely on manual monitoring and subjective assessments, resulting in delayed detection of diseases and pests, inefficient soil management, and increased production costs.

Small- and medium-scale farmers lack affordable tools to access timely, data-driven insights to optimize their farming practices — a gap this project directly addresses.

💡 Proposed Solution ▼

The Smart Agriculture Assistant is a comprehensive AI-powered solution integrating three main modules: an IoT sensor network for soil data collection, an image recognition module for pest and disease detection, and a cloud-based data processing platform.

The system automates crop health diagnostics and soil condition monitoring, enabling real-time detection of pest infestations, diseases, and unfavorable soil pH levels — targeting high-value crops like pineapple but adaptable to various crops.

Farmers receive actionable insights through a user-friendly mobile or web dashboard, empowering them to make timely interventions and reduce crop losses.

🏗️ System Architecture ▼

IoT Sensor Layer: Robotic arm deployed in the field monitors soil pH, moisture, and nutrient levels. Camera captures crop images periodically.

Gateway: Aggregates data from sensors and cameras, ensuring secure transmission to the cloud engine.

Cloud AI Engine: Processes data using machine learning models — CNN for disease detection, predictive models for pest forecasting, explainable AI for growth monitoring.

User Dashboard: Displays crop health status, soil condition alerts, and personalized treatment suggestions via mobile or web interface with voice output in local languages.

🗂️ Domain Expertise & Data ▼

This project requires interdisciplinary expertise spanning agricultural science, machine learning & computer vision, IoT systems, and cloud engineering.

  • Historical and real-time soil condition measurements (pH, moisture, nutrients)
  • Annotated images of pineapple plants with common diseases and pest damage
  • Local climate data to correlate environmental factors with crop health trends

Collaboration with local farmers, agricultural extension officers, and research institutes is vital to gather domain knowledge and validate AI predictions.

🌍 SDG Alignment & Impact ▼

The expected outcome is an economically viable, easy-to-use tool that supports:

  • SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth: empowering small-scale farmers with affordable precision agriculture tools
  • SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure: leveraging AI and IoT to innovate Sri Lankan agricultural infrastructure

This project innovates by combining affordable sensors with AI algorithms tailored for local crops and farming conditions, ensuring practical adoption and scalability.

System Components

Four Core Modules

Each team member owns a dedicated module, contributing unique AI capabilities to the integrated platform.

1

IoT Soil Monitoring

Mobile robotic arm with calibrated pH, moisture, and nutrient sensors. Wireless IoT transmission with instant voice output for hands-free farmer feedback.

2

AI Plant Disease Detection

CNN-based model trained on localized Sri Lankan crop disease images. Offline-capable on mobile devices with voice-based diagnosis for low-literacy users.

3

Pest Outbreak Prediction

Predictive AI fuses image analysis with environmental inputs. Real-time spoken risk alerts warn farmers of mealybug or pest outbreaks before damage occurs.

4

Stunted Growth Monitoring

Grad-CAM explainable CNNs analyze farmer-submitted photos. Crowdsourced network detects regional threats and delivers voice alerts in native Sinhala/Tamil.
